# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, professional, and harassment-free community.

Be respectful, assume good intent, give constructive feedback, and focus discussions on improving
the project. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, or publication of another
person's private information is not acceptable.

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository maintainers. Maintainers may remove
content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
